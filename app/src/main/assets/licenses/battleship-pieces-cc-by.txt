Original Battleship Pieces by MZimb
https://www.thingiverse.com/thing:5190846

The Battleship hulls and hit/miss pins in this app are adapted from that model.
The hulls were scaled onto the classic fleet. Measured by length, the pack's
Aircraft Carrier is the carrier (5), its Cruiser is the battleship (4), its
Destroyer is the cruiser (3), its Sub is the submarine (3), and its Mine
Sweeper is the destroyer (2). Pegs under the hulls were left off so the ships
sit on the water.
