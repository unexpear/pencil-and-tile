ENABLE (Enhanced North American Benchmark Lexicon)

The ENABLE word list is in the public domain. It was compiled by Alan Beale from
public-domain sources for use in word games. No attribution is legally required.
Credit: Alan Beale and the ENABLE contributors.

Pencil & Tile uses it as the playable list for letter games. tools/words/build_dictionary.py
drops words shorter than 2 letters, longer than 15 letters, and words named in
tools/words/blocked.txt (profanity, slurs, sexual terms, and the same simple
inflections). The English lexicon marks that same shipped list as playable.

The builder downloads enable1.txt from https://norvig.com/ngrams/enable1.txt
and checks it still covers that list.
