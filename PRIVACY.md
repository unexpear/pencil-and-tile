# Pencil & Tile: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 October 2026

Pencil & Tile ("the app") is made by TryingToDo ("we"). This policy explains what the app does with
information. The short version: **the app collects nothing, and it has no way to send anything.**

## What the app stores

To let you continue games and see your records, the app saves on your device:

- games in progress (puzzles, moves, notes, time played and hints used),
- finished games, best times, scores and wins,
- your settings (language, theme, timer and hint options) and any custom Sudoku grids or themes you make.

For Word Meaning, the answers you type are judged on your device by a small built-in language model. Your
answers are saved only as part of your game on the device.

## What the app sends

Nothing. The app does not request internet access, has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no
in-app purchases, and it does not share information with us or anyone else.

## Backups

If Android backup is switched on in your device settings, Android may include the app's saved games and
records in your device backup (for example, Google's backup service) and restore them to a new device.
That backup is handled by Android and your backup provider under their terms; we never receive it. You can
turn backup off in your device's settings.

## Deleting your information

You can clear finished games and high scores, or every started and finished game, in the app
(Settings → Your data). You can remove everything by clearing the app's storage in Android settings
or uninstalling the app.

## Children

The app contains no ads, accounts, chat or purchases and does not collect personal information from anyone,
including children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. The app itself will not start
collecting information without an update that says so first.
