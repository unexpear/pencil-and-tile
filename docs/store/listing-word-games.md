# Pencil & Tile: "Word games" custom store listing

A custom store listing (Play Console → Grow users → Store listings → Create custom store listing) shown to
people who find the app by searching for word games. It duplicates the default listing and swaps the text
and screenshots so word games lead.

- Reference name: `Word games search`
- Audience: Search keyword → `word games`, `word puzzle`, `word game offline`, `crossword`, `word search`,
  `word battle`. Avoid other companies' game names.
- Rollout: 50% at first (Play's advice), then 100% once it performs at least as well as the default.
- Phone screenshots, in this order: `screenshots/words-1-wordsworn.png`, `words-2-sprawl.png`,
  `words-3-loneletter.png`, then the existing `phone-3-common-threads.png`, `phone-4-five-letters.png` and
  `phone-5-word-meaning.png`.
- Publish it with release 1.1.0, when these games are in the app.

Same limits as the main listing: `python docs/store/check_listing.py listing-word-games.md`.

## en-US

Title: Pencil & Tile

Short description: Word games offline: battle monsters, chain letters, stitch quilts. No ads.

Full description:
Word games that play completely offline, with no ads, no account and no tracking. Free and open source, forever.

WORD GAMES
• Wordsworn: spell words from letter tiles to battle a run of monsters. Long words and rare letters hit harder
• Letter Sprawl: chain touching letters into words; longer words score more
• Lone Letter: one letter, many categories. Beat computer players who might think of the same answer
• Word Quilt: place each patch's letters so every row and column reads as words
• Blotwords: ink the grid with mysterious command words you work out for yourself
• Common Threads: sort sixteen words into four hidden groups, every puzzle proven to have one answer
• Five Letters: find the hidden word in a few guesses
• Word Meaning: explain a word from its sentence; your answer is judged right on your phone
• Crossword, Word search, Hangman, Cryptogram, Word scramble, Acrostic, Code cracker and Dropquote

• Minesweeper, Checkers, Reversi, Dots and Boxes, Sprouts, Magnetic cluster, Connect Four, Mastermind, 2048 and Tetras

MADE TO BE PLAYED YOUR WAY

• Four difficulty levels, timed or untimed play, and a record of your wins
• Hints that are free, or earned by playing if you like a challenge
• Themes and dark mode, and screen-reader support
