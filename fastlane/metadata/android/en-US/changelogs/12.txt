New games: Tic-Tac-Toe, Tri-D Chess, and Raumschach.

Tic-Tac-Toe on 3×3, 4×4, or 5×5. The first to fill a row, column, or diagonal wins. Play the computer, or pass the phone.

Tri-D Chess follows Charles Roth's Federation Revised Standard 5.0. Three fixed main boards and four attack boards that slide one or two ranks. Checkmate wins.

Raumschach is the 5×5×5 cube kept by the International Raumschach Federation, with a unicorn. Checkmate wins.
