Five new word games! Wordsworn: spell words to battle a run of monsters. Letter Sprawl: chain touching letters into words. Word Quilt: place each patch's letters so every row and column is a word. Lone Letter: one letter, many categories, against computer players. Blotwords: ink the grid with strange command words and work out what they do, with themes you can make yourself. Variants now sit under their game on the home screen.
