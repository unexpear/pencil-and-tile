New game: Rock Paper Scissors on a hex board. Each side starts with the same pieces and moves one hex onto an empty hex or an adjacent enemy it beats. Paper beats rock, rock beats scissors, and scissors beats paper. Landing captures. No jumping. Win by taking every enemy piece or leaving no legal move. Boards are 5×5 and 7×7. Play the computer, or pass the phone.

Computer opponents are stronger across the board games. Easy slips. Medium and above take a forced win and avoid hanging a piece.
