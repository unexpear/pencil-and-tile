Pencil & Tile is a quiet collection of 38 classic and original puzzles that play completely offline. No ads, no account, no tracking: just puzzles. Free and open source, forever.

NUMBER PUZZLES
• Sudoku in 8 variants and 4 sizes up to 16×16, plus Killer Sudoku and a custom grid editor that checks every grid has exactly one solution
• Samurai Sudoku, Calcudoku, Kakuro, Futoshiki, Nonograms and Hitori

WORD GAMES
• Common Threads: sort sixteen words into four hidden groups; every puzzle is built from rules and proven to have one answer
• Five Letters: find the hidden word in a few guesses
• Word Meaning: explain a word from its sentence; your answer is judged right on your phone
• Wordsworn: spell words from letter tiles to battle a run of monsters
• Letter Sprawl: chain touching letters into words; longer words score more
• Word Quilt: place each patch's letters so every row and column reads as words
• Lone Letter: one letter, many categories, against computer players who might think of the same answer
• Blotwords: ink the grid with mysterious command words you work out for yourself
• Crossword, Word search, Hangman, Cryptogram, Word scramble, Acrostic, Code cracker and Dropquote

CARDS AND TILES
• Mahjong Solitaire, Klondike, Spider and Pyramid Solitaire, Dominoes and Memory

BOARD AND ARCADE
• Minesweeper, Checkers, Reversi, Dots and Boxes, Sprouts and Magnetic cluster against the computer
• Connect Four: drop discs on a 7×6 board and line up four
• 2048 and Tetras falling blocks

MADE TO BE PLAYED YOUR WAY
• Every game has its own short guided tutorial and full rules; tutorials are always optional
• Four difficulty levels, timed or untimed play, best times and a record of your wins
• Pause any game and pick it up later
• Hints that are free, or earned by playing if you like a challenge
• Themes and dark mode, zoom and pan for big boards, and screen-reader support
• In English, 简体中文, 日本語, Español and Deutsch

Your games and records stay on your device.
